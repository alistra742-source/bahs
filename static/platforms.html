How each sniper is read

A platform is only ever called free on its own explicit answer — a 404, an empty result list, a page that says the name is unclaimed. A refusal (403/429), a challenge page, or a body without the field we look at becomes a refusal or an unreadable answer and is retried; it never becomes a verdict. A free verdict is then re-asked once on a different exit before it is reported.
